Hourglass · Combat Prototype

RPG Game

A tale of one adventurer, told across two dangerous rounds of the world.

How this prototype works

At each round, draw tokens into an eight-token reserve. Select a token, then choose an action to assign it. Completed actions resolve when you resolve the round; incomplete actions hold their tokens. Speed adds initiative, Health adds healing/potency, and every Critical token must pass a 50% flip or the whole action fails.
